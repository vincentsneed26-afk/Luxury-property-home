About This Property

A distinguished riverside
townhouse of rare distinction

Set on one of London's most celebrated addresses, this magnificent Grade II listed townhouse presents an extraordinary opportunity to acquire a truly exceptional family home. Positioned on the Chelsea Embankment overlooking the River Thames, the property commands breathtaking views across to Battersea and beyond.

Arranged over six floors and extending to approximately 5,840 sq ft, the house has been comprehensively restored and renovated to an exceptional standard by its current owners, who have seamlessly combined period character with the finest contemporary finishes and technology.

The principal reception rooms are of outstanding proportions, featuring original cornicing, fireplaces and panelling, alongside discreet integrated audio-visual systems and bespoke joinery throughout. The lower ground floor has been transformed into a stunning entertainment suite with a bespoke kitchen, wine cellar, and direct access to the walled garden beyond.
